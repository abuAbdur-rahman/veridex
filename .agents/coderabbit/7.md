### 4. `apps/server/src/services/import.service.ts:225`

**Reconfirming an import marks a successful job as failed.**

`uploadSpreadsheet` stores the job with `status: "completed"` after parsing; the worker also sets `status: "completed"` and `parsedRows: null` after a successful insert run.
